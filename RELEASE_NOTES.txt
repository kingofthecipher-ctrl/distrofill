DistroFill 1.1.7

Your release, ready when you return.

• Local draft autosave now restores dropped tracks, metadata edits, unfinished bulk edits, and your selected tab when you reopen DistroFill or restart Chrome.
• Open DistroFill in Chrome’s side panel using the arrow below Fill. Your popup and panel share the same draft.
• Refreshed light and dark themes, clearer controls, and aligned theme and license buttons.
• Animated gradients on the Fill button and header divider, with reduced-motion support.
• Fixed restoration of intentionally empty fields and refreshing after stripping comments.

Security fixes
• Restricted form filling to validated HTTPS DistroKid destinations, including a second check if the active tab navigates during a fill.
• Fixed unsafe rendering of audio titles and export-folder names so names display as text instead of being interpreted as HTML.
• Removed full license-verification response logging to avoid exposing customer/license details in console output.

Audio processing and saved audio drafts remain on your device. Clear tracks removes the saved track draft. Existing subscriptions and activation behavior are unchanged.

Requires Chrome 116 or later. This update adds the sidePanel permission for the optional side panel.
